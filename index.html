Theoretical Foundations: Modern Psychological Perspectives I

Investigating the Biological, Behavioral, Cognitive, and Psychodynamic perspectives; analyzing perception, learning, reinforcement, memory, and unconscious processes in consumer decision-making.

5-Min Consumer Behavior Warm-Up

When a consumer buys a smartphone, why do they make that choice? A neuroscientist looks at brain dopamine loops (Biological); a marketer offering loyalty points relies on habit conditioning (Behavioral); an analyst looks at mental memory retrieval of reviews (Cognitive); while a luxury brand expert examines hidden status desires (Psychodynamic). Modern marketing requires synthesizing these four complementary lenses.

1. Biological Perspective

Focuses on brain structures, neurotransmitters, genetics, and physiological responses driving perception and consumer drives.

Key Concept: Brain & Physiology

2. Behavioral Perspective

Emphasizes environmental stimuli, observable learning, reinforcement schedules, and habit formation in brand loyalty.

Key Concept: Reinforcement

3. Cognitive Perspective

Examines internal mental processes such as memory encoding, attention, information processing, and decision heuristics.

Key Concept: Memory & Schema

4. Psychodynamic Perspective

Explores unconscious motives, childhood conflicts, defense mechanisms, and hidden symbolic desires behind purchases.

Key Concept: Unconscious Processes
